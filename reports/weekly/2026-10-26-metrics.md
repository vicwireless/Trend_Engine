## What the Etsy API cannot provide

- Daily views or visits as Etsy reports them (only lifetime `views` per listing; daily = snapshot delta)
- Traffic sources, search terms, Pinterest referrals, conversion rate (Shop Stats only)
- Etsy Ads spend, impressions, clicks, ROAS (no Ads API)
- Whether the owner ticked the AI-generator field in the listing editor (not in the API; the description sentence is checked instead)
- Pins posted or pin clicks (no Pinterest token; Pinterest hosts are blocked in the cloud env)
- Fees and net payout per listing (payment ledger is shop-level; not computed yet)

Use the weekly Shop Stats CSV export (`data/stats_exports/`) for visits, sources and ads.
